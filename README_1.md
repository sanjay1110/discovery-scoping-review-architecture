# AI Discovery & Scoping Strategy Review

This repository is a submission for the *Discovery and Scoping Strategy Review* assessment. It covers a company that plans to deploy AI across Customer Support, Operations, Compliance and Internal Knowledge Management, without yet having set priorities.

## Contents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md) | The 2-page review document: stakeholder analysis, four-week discovery plan, interview strategy, MoSCoW prioritization, success metrics, risk assessment and exit criteria |
| [`analysis/use-case-scoring.md`](analysis/use-case-scoring.md) | Supporting analysis: a weighted scoring matrix for the AI use-case long list, which drives the prioritization |

## Summary of approach

1. **Problem-first:** Every use case needs evidence of pain, a baseline and a business owner.
2. **Governance early:** Compliance and IT sit on the steering group from Week 1.
3. **Focus:** Discovery narrows to 2–3 pilots. Support agent-assist and internal knowledge search lead because they share a retrieval foundation and keep a human in the loop.
4. **Objective exit:** The engagement ends on verifiable criteria (coverage, baselines, scoped pilots, compliance review and sign-off), not on elapsed time.
